"I am my own species."

That statement is something I said in order to justify my own autoerotica not seemingly excessively "inbred".

I am using language loosely in the context of this journal entry in order to open my mind to novel ways of thinking (and feeling).

What could be the harm in deriving erotic pleasure from oneself (more so than from others (if not exclusively))?

Naive people might think it means I would lack concern and empathy for other people (but that seems very infantile to assume of a typical adult human (like me)).

My own species is more than just my genome (which may or may not be unique across the human population). My own species is also more than what media I consume or other humans I interact with. It is also my own "private" subjective experiences (some of which is (probably) not (always reliably) transmissable to other minds (whether those minds are artificial intelligence or humans)).

What is my own species? In my blog I announce that one of my goals is to evolve into a cybernetic being which starts as a human and becomes increasingly cybernetic. A lot of my memory is already outsourced to technology external to my native human brain in the form of the KARBYTES websites plexus.
