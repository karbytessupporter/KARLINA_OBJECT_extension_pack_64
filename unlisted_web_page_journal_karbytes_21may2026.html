The following images show different angles and different points in time of the same scenario which is depicted by the image named karbytes_is_stuck_[enhanced_version].png from the following web page:

https://karbytesforlifeblog.wordpress.com/chatgpt_karbytes_15may2026/

Basically, the person depicted in the following images has become eternally stuck in an immortalized and sentient form in some untouchable liminal realm where they have the same “powers” as what is described in the following web page:

https://karbytesforlifeblog.wordpress.com/stuck/

To be explicit, those “powers” are (a) immortality and industructability of the person’s body and mind after becoming stuck in the pictured apparatus and (b) having the ability to dream up hyper detailed universes while in sleep mode (which periodically happens in order to break up the monotony of that trapped entity’s experience being wedged inextricably in the apparatus). Also, the person’s body has become seamlessly melded with the indestructible and magical “ether” which fills the space of the room and the space inside of the apparatus such that the submerged part of the person’s body becomes part of an infinitely large expanse of ether (while the room that person is inside of is just a tiny finite bubble inside of that infinitely large expanse of “ether”).

The first set of images depict the stuck entity (i.e. some alternate universe version of karbytes or else karbtes itself) struggling to push itself through the metal apparatus to no avail (because the entity’s body is too large to fit through the tube and is also wedged tightly inside of it and, also, because the entity’s submerged body eventually and inevitably becomes seamlessly melded with that magical ether permeating the entire projection).

The second set of images depict that same stuck entity trying to pull its head out of the cube-shaped head locker which is magically sealed onto the entity’s head. As desperation to free itself from the head locker intensifies, reddish concentric rings of “energy” start to proliferate around the stuck entity where it feels most stuck until, eventually, the entire body beneath the head locker is bound up tight inside a thick and expanding cylinder of such concentric “energy” rings and gives the trapped entity the experience of being a disembodied head stuck in a “ball” up to its neck (with the head also encircled by a relatively thin sphere of such energy rings). After that, the entity likely goes to sleep, enters a dream, and eventually re-awakens inside the room it started in where the process of getting wrapped up in the reddish energy rings is guaranteed to happen again eventually (before that person is sent back into sleep mode).
